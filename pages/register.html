Please read carefully before creating your KAYA account.

1. Agreement to Terms

By creating an account on KAYA, you agree to be bound by these Terms and Conditions and our Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, you may not use our platform. KAYA reserves the right to update these terms at any time, with notice sent to your registered email address.

2. Eligibility & Residency Requirement

You must be at least 18 years old to register on KAYA. By registering, you confirm that:

  • You are at least 18 years of age.
  • You will provide accurate, current, and complete information.
  • You will maintain the security of your account credentials.
  • You accept full responsibility for all activity under your account.

📍 Minglanilla Residents Only. KAYA is an exclusive platform for residents and businesses of Minglanilla, Cebu. Registration is open only to individuals residing in one of the 19 official barangays of Minglanilla (Cadulawan, Calajo-an, Camp 7, Camp 8, Cuanos, Guindaruhan, Linao, Manduang, Pakigne, Poblacion Ward I–IV, Tubod, Tulay, Tunghaan, Tungkil, Tungkop, and Vito).

After registration, you will be required to upload a valid government-issued ID or Barangay Certificate confirming your Minglanilla address. Your account will remain pending until an KAYA admin reviews and approves your proof of residency.

📋 4. How We Use Your Personal Data

  • To create and manage your KAYA account.
  • To match job seekers with relevant job opportunities and clients.
  • To display your profile to potential employers or clients.
  • To send job alerts, platform updates, and service notifications.
  • To improve platform performance, features, and user experience.
  • To comply with applicable laws and prevent fraud or abuse.

⚠️ Important: Your profile information — including your name, skills, and profile photo — will be visible to other platform users (clients or job seekers depending on your role). You control what information you choose to add to your profile.

5. Data Sharing & Third Parties

KAYA does not sell your personal data to third parties. We may share data only in the following circumstances:

  • With employers or clients you apply to or contact through KAYA.
  • With service providers who help operate our platform (e.g., Supabase for database hosting).
  • When required by law or to protect the rights and safety of users.

6. Data Retention & Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access — view all personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct — update inaccurate information via your profile.
  • Delete — request account deletion; we will remove your data within 30 days.
  • Withdraw consent — you may close your account at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@kaya.ph.

Violations may result in immediate account suspension or termination.
